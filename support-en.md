# Track my Watch support

Have a question or a problem with Track my Watch? Email [trackmywatch.support@gmail.com](mailto:trackmywatch.support@gmail.com).

Please include your app version, iOS version, device model and the steps that lead to the problem. You can find the app version in Track my Watch's settings.

Please do not send passwords or complete private backups. Check screenshots for personal information before sending them.

## Where is my data stored?

Track my Watch stores your collection locally in the app's storage on your device. You do not need an account. The app does not automatically sync your collection to other devices.

A backup saved to iCloud Drive or another file provider is a separate backup file. iOS device backups also depend on your system settings.

## How do I save a backup?

1. Open **Settings → Backup & Restore**.
2. Enter a new password of at least **8 characters** twice.
3. Tap **Create backup** and wait until the file is ready.
4. Tap **Save backup** and choose a location in the Files app.
5. Wait for confirmation that the file has been saved.

The backup includes private details, photos and documents. Keep the password safe and separate from the file. A long, unique password offers better protection.

## How do I restore a backup?

1. Open **Settings → Backup & Restore**.
2. Enter the file's original password in the first field. You do not need to repeat it to restore a backup.
3. Tap **Choose and verify backup** and select your backup file.
4. Check the preview and confirm restoration.

Missing watches are added. Watches whose IDs already exist are skipped. Existing watches are not overwritten or returned to an earlier state. If every watch already exists, there is nothing to add.

## What if I forget my backup password?

The password cannot be reset. The developer cannot open the backup without the matching password either. If your collection is still in the app, you can create a new backup with a new password.

## Why are private details or other contents missing?

Empty fields are not displayed. **Discreet mode** also hides private information. Check it under **Settings → Privacy**. Your saved information is preserved.

Tap the headings **Details**, **Purchase details** or **Brand distribution** to expand or collapse their contents.

## How do I see a complete photo?

Tap the photo in the gallery. Full-screen view shows the complete saved photo against a black background. Pinch or double-tap to zoom in. Double-tap again to show the whole photo; **Done** closes the view.

In the gallery, the photo fills its image area, so its edges may be cropped.

## Can I hide wear hours?

Yes. Turn off **Show wear hours** in Settings. Wear days and recorded sessions are preserved, and tracking continues. Turning off **Wear tracking** disables the entire feature and ends running sessions.

## What does a Watch Card include?

A Watch Card includes the photo and the watch details intended for the card. Purchase price, serial number, storage location and private notes are not included as fields. Check your photo and freely entered text for personal information before sharing.
